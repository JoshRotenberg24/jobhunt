## Top Gaps

1. **HubSpot depth.** The JD wants ownership of HubSpot specifically. Josh's primary CRM-build platform is GoHighLevel; HubSpot appears in the profile as a secondary tool (used for client record-keeping, some builds) rather than as the system he's administered end to end. The resume is honest about this ("GoHighLevel primary, hands-on HubSpot builds") rather than overstating it — worth being ready to speak to in an interview, and worth a genuine "how transferable is GHL admin to HubSpot admin" answer.
2. **No bid, quotation, or proposal experience anywhere in the profile.** This is a named responsibility in the posting (organizing incomplete information, standardizing documentation, moving opportunities through defined stages) and there's nothing to point to. It's a real gap, not just an unevidenced one.
3. **Zero heavy-equipment, construction, infrastructure, or industrial-vertical experience.** His B2B background is agencies, professional services, and SaaS/MarTech. This is the largest driver of the low domain-alignment score. It's not disqualifying for an *operations and systems* role (the posting is explicit this isn't a sales role), but a recruiter may reasonably ask why this vertical.
4. **Formal "Manager" scope with direct reports is thin.** Josh has led a team (Wix) and owned a business as founder, but hasn't held a corporate "Manager" title with a standing team reporting to him. Not likely disqualifying given the founder/ownership story, but worth being ready to address directly.
