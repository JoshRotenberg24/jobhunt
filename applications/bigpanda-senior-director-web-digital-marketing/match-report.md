## The core issue, stated plainly

This is a Senior Director-level role that explicitly wants someone who has "owned this function before, not just contributed to it." It asks for demonstrated senior ownership of a company's entire digital marketing function: setting company-wide SEO/AEO/GEO strategy, leading a full website redesign end to end, owning a performance-marketing budget with pipeline/ROI accountability, owning website security and GDPR compliance, and running an external agency ecosystem as the primary execution model, reporting directly to senior leadership as the internal authority.

Josh has 15+ years in B2B SaaS and MarTech, but every one of those years has been as an individual contributor (Account Executive, Account Manager, Channel Partnerships) or as a solo founder running his own consulting practice (Solenzo) for small service businesses. He has never held a Director- or VP-level in-house marketing leadership title, never led a full website redesign, never owned a company's security/GDPR posture, and has not managed an external agency ecosystem as another company's own outsourced execution arm. This isn't a missing tool or a missing certification; it's a different job than the one he's had.
