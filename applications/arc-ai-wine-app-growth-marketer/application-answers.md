## App-install campaign experience, strategy, and creative direction

I run paid campaigns hands-on in Meta Ads Manager across Facebook and Instagram, I'm Meta Ads certified, and I've run app-install and TikTok campaign work alongside my web conversion campaigns. At Level Agency I managed paid media for accounts spending $20K+ a month, peaking above $100K, owning budget planning and A/B testing of creative and audiences.

My strategy starts with measurement. Before spend scales, I confirm the conversion events are firing correctly (Pixel or SDK, plus the downstream events that signal a valuable user, not just an install), so the algorithm optimizes toward users who actually engage. From there I launch a small set of distinct creative angles rather than minor variations, give each enough budget to read, and shift spend to what's converting.

For creative direction, I write the hooks and copy myself and brief creative around one clear promise per ad. For a wine app, that means testing angles like "never guess at a wine list again" against the experience of the app itself, in a native, phone-first format that looks like content rather than an ad, especially on TikTok and Reels. I keep a simple log of what each test taught us so every round of creative gets sharper on a startup budget.
