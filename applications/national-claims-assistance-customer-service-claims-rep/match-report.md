## Read this first: what this role actually is

- **1099, commission only, no base pay.** No benefits or payroll taxes withheld. The $35K-$150K range is a claim about what other contractors earn, not a guarantee.
- **The real job is signing homeowners.** Public adjusters get paid a percentage of the claim settlement. "Unlimited income," "ambitious," and pay tied to performance mean the rep is paid for getting homeowners to sign on, then documenting the damage. It's a sales role with a customer-service title.
- **It doesn't match Josh's stated preferences.** He prefers taking over existing accounts to building new ones from scratch, and his salary baseline is $75K base. Unknown: whether the firm supplies leads (e.g., storm-driven inbound) or reps prospect for themselves. That's the key question for the Zoom overview.
- **Licensing exam:** a Colorado public adjuster license is required. Ask who pays for the pre-licensing course and exam fees, and whether pay starts before the license is issued.

None of these rule him out. They're for Josh to weigh.
