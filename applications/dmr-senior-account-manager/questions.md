On the paid side I work in Google Ads on targeting, search terms, and lead quality. Getting traffic to convert is only half of it. Making sure the lead is then handled properly is the other half, and most agencies hand that part off.

## Q4. Briefly describe a time you retained, expanded, or turned around a client account. What actions did you take, and what was the outcome?

A client's Google Ads account was producing volume but poor lead quality, with spam and unqualified inquiries clogging the pipeline. I rebuilt the targeting and negative keyword structure to cut the junk, and click-through quality improved.

The decision that mattered came next. The obvious move was to keep optimizing the ads, since that is what I had been brought in for. But looking at what happened after the click, the traffic was landing on a site that was not built to convert it. More time in the ad account would have bought better clicks feeding the same leak. So I told the client the remaining bottleneck was on-site conversion, not media, and that the next round of work belonged there instead.

That conversation is the harder part of the job. Telling a client the problem is not the thing they hired you to fix only works if you bring the evidence and a sequenced plan, not just an observation.

The outcome was cleaner lead flow into the pipeline and a clear, evidenced case for where the next investment should go.

Separately, on portfolio retention: at Birdeye I managed 15 to 20 reseller partners with deals from $10K to $45K and retained all but one, roughly 94%, by watching utilization and engagement metrics for adoption gaps and intervening before an account went quiet.
