## Top gaps (be ready for these in the vet)
- **No UX title, ever.** Every piece of research he has done was inside an account
  management, onboarding, or growth role. A screener scanning for "UX Researcher" in a
  job title will not find one. The resume headline is an honest self-description, not a
  claimed past title.
- **Journey maps and accessibility standards are not in his background.** Scope item 5
  names journey maps and item 6 names accessibility. He has personas and findings
  reports, but has not claimed either of those two. Do not bluff them in the interview.
- **Not a practicing UX/UI designer.** He makes structure and layout decisions informed
  by behavior data. He does not work in Figma or produce design files. One of the four
  Required Skills is therefore adjacent rather than direct.
- **No formal research vocabulary or artifacts.** He has never written a research plan, a
  screener, or a discussion guide by those names. The underlying work is real; the
  terminology is not habitual. Worth 30 minutes of prep before any interview so the
  language matches the practice.
