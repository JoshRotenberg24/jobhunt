# Application Answers — Sales Enablement Manager, Ookla

## What is your experience with Ookla's products?

My experience with Speedtest and Downdetector is as an end user, not a professional one.
I've used Speedtest for years to check connection speed and compare ISP performance, and
Downdetector to figure out whether a client-side issue was on my end or a vendor's. I
haven't used Ekahau or RootMetrics, those sit inside network engineering and carrier
testing, outside anything I've done hands-on.

What I bring is the buyer's-eye view: I know what it's like to rely on Speedtest's data to
make a real decision. That's useful context for building enablement material aimed at the
people selling into that same audience, even without professional experience on the
product side.
