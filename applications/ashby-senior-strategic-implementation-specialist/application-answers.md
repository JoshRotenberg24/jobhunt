## Most complex product you've helped customers implement (3-4 sentences)

Accelo, a CRM, project management, and billing platform for agencies and professional services firms, including architecture and engineering firms. It was complex because one system replaced several, connecting sales, projects, time tracking, and invoicing, so a change in one module rippled into the others and every team had to adopt it at once. My strategy was to sequence the work: I built an implementation plan ranking which systems to change and in what order, so each team had a stable foundation before the next layer went live. I paired that with workshops for each client team showing how their part of the product would work, then handed the plan to the implementation team to execute.
