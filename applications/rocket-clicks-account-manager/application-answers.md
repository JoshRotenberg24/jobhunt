# Rocket Clicks application answers (drafts, Oct 12 2026)

## What specifically about this role tells you it's the right one for you?

I do my best work in the strategic conversation with an owner, talking through pricing, intake, and capacity, and this role is built around exactly that. I've co-owned a business and I run my own practice now, so I've had those numbers be mine, not just my client's.

I also know how professional-services firms operate. At Accelo I sold operations software to agencies and professional-services firms, and I had to learn how each one priced work, staffed projects, and billed clients. I see family law firms running on the same levers.

And I want the depth that comes from one practice area. I build intake and booking systems for service businesses, and I see the same leaks again and again: slow follow-up, no-shows, consultations that never turn into signed work. I know that seeing a pattern across a dozen firms is what makes my recommendation credible to an owner, and that's the work I want to be doing.
