# Rocket Clicks application answers (drafts, Oct 12 2026)

## What specifically about this role tells you it's the right one for you?

Three things. First, you've separated the strategic conversation from delivery, so the account manager's job is to sit with the owner and talk about pricing, intake, and capacity, not chase deliverables. That's the conversation I'm best in. I've co-owned a business and I run my own practice now, so I've had those numbers be mine, not just my client's.

Second, the focus on professional-services firms. At Accelo I sold operations software to agencies and professional-services firms, and the job was learning how each one priced work, staffed projects, and billed clients. Family law firms run on the same levers.

Third, the single practice area. In my own work building intake and booking systems for service businesses, the same leaks show up again and again: slow follow-up, no-shows, consultations that never turn into signed work. Seeing that pattern across a dozen firms in one niche is what makes a recommendation credible to an owner, and that's the kind of work I want to be doing.
