# Cash App / Block: Application Answers

## Why are you applying? What excited you about this role?

I've spent my career selling to business owners on the thing they care about most: more customers and a better conversion rate. Cash App Pay and Afterpay sell exactly that, with a built-in audience of 50+ million people most merchants can't reach on their own. I've built online storefronts for retail brands and sold to local merchants, so I know how much a smoother checkout is worth to them. This role lets me run full-cycle deals with brands I'd actually want to win, and put a product in front of them that pays for itself.

## Block's purpose is economic empowerment. What does that mean to you?

To me it means taking the friction out of money so people can get on with their lives. I run a small business, and I've spent years working with owners who lose customers and hours to clunky processes that bigger companies never think about. Economic empowerment is making buying, paying, and getting paid simple enough that the person on either side of the counter doesn't have to worry about the transaction and can focus on what they're actually trying to do.
