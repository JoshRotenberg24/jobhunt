Functionally, this is one of Josh's strongest matches this session, CRM ownership, sales-ops data accuracy, and cross-team coordination are exactly what his last several roles have been built around. But the posting's title ("Coordinator"), its 1+ year experience bar, and its $55K–$72K range all signal a role calibrated for someone earlier in their career, not 15+ years in with founder-level CRM ownership.

This isn't a fit problem, it's a leveling and compensation question worth Josh weighing on his own terms: is he open to a coordinator-level title and this pay band in exchange for something else (equity, company stage, remote flexibility, a foot in the door at a company he's excited about), or does he want to hold out for a role priced at his actual experience level. Nothing here should be read as "don't apply", just "know what you're applying to."
