# Clipster, Growth Marketer: application answers

## Why are you a strong fit for this role?

I've been doing this exact job for my own business. At Solenzo I'm the only marketer, so I own SEO, email, organic content, landing pages, and copy end to end, with nobody above me checking the work. I run SEO hands-on: technical audits in Screaming Frog, keyword and competitor research, schema, and on-page fixes I implement myself. On paid search, I've cleaned up a client's Google Ads account to cut spam leads and improve click quality, then traced the remaining drop-off to the landing page.

I also measure what each channel actually produces. I built a free audit offer and promoted it through LinkedIn content; 180+ businesses enrolled and 2 became paying clients. That's the kind of number I'd want to know for every Clipster channel, so we can scale what works and stop what doesn't.

Before Solenzo I spent 15+ years in B2B SaaS and agency roles, writing landing pages, email sequences, and ad copy, and explaining software clearly enough that people acted on it. Turning word of mouth into demand you can count and repeat is the job I want.
