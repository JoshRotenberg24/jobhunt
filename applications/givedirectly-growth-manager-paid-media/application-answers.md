# GiveDirectly, Growth Manager Paid Media: application answers

## Why are you interested in this role at GiveDirectly? (200 words max)

Two things pulled me in. First, the shape of the job. You want someone to build a paid program without an agency, stand up attribution so spend can be judged outside the ad platforms, and make budget calls from their own data. That's how I already work. At Solenzo I'm the whole marketing function for my clients and for my own business, so I build the campaigns, wire up the tracking, and decide what to cut.

Second, the organization. GiveDirectly is unusually candid about evidence: you publish research, you measure what cash actually does, and your values ask people to admit when something isn't working. That's the culture I want to do marketing in, where the question is "did this produce donors" rather than "did this look good in a report."

I'm also drawn to the monthly giving goal. Most of my career has been about keeping people, not just acquiring them, and growing a recurring donor base is that same problem with a better purpose behind it.

## Read the "What you'll do" section and tell us why that is you (200 words max)

What isn't obvious from my resume is how much of the supporter journey I build end to end. At Solenzo I connect landing pages and forms to the CRM, then run automated SMS and email sequences that nurture, convert, and bring people back. That's the same path as ad, donation page, email, CRM, and monthly retention, and I've built it more than once.

I'm also disciplined about not buying my way out of a conversion problem. On one client's Google Ads account, I cut spam leads and improved click quality, then found the real bottleneck was the landing page. More spend would have hidden that.

I set up the measurement myself: Google Tag Manager on every site I build, GA4 tracking, and dashboards in Power BI and GA4 with SQL behind them. I write ad copy and landing pages and direct the creative rather than waiting on a team.

And I'm used to reporting up. At Level Agency I delivered QBRs on ROI, CAC, LTV, and conversion to senior stakeholders, with clear recommendations on where budget should go next.

The honest gap is scale: I haven't managed a $5M budget. I'd build the foundation that earns one.
