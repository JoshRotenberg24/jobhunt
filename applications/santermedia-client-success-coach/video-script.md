**1. Tell us about yourself**

I'm Josh Rotenberg. I run Solenzo, my own marketing and automation company, and before that I spent about fifteen years in client-facing roles at SaaS and agency companies, mostly account management, onboarding, and customer success. At Solenzo I work directly with service-business owners, building out their lead capture, appointment booking, and follow-up systems in GoHighLevel. So a lot of my day to day is exactly this: sitting with an owner, looking at what's working and what isn't, and holding them to the plan we set.

**2. Fitness industry / gym experience**

I haven't worked inside a gym or a fitness business directly, so I want to be straight about that up front. What I have done, at Solenzo and before that at Birdeye, is build and run the exact systems SanterMedia installs for gym owners: lead capture, appointment booking with reminders, no-show recovery, and review generation, just for other local service businesses. The problems are the same shape. An owner who's great at the service side and needs someone else owning the follow-up and the numbers. That's the seat I've been sitting in for years, just for plumbers and electricians instead of gyms.

**3. A time you held a client accountable / a hard conversation for their benefit**

This came up constantly managing channel partners at Birdeye. Part of my job was watching adoption and usage data across fifteen to twenty partner accounts, and when I saw a partner's engagement start slipping, the easy move is to wait until renewal and let that be the hard conversation. I didn't do that. I'd bring the data to them early, show exactly what was dropping and why it mattered for their results, and lay out what needed to change. It's not a comfortable call, owners don't love hearing they're underusing something they're paying for, but it's the one that actually protects the relationship instead of just avoiding friction. I held onto about ninety four percent of that partner book, and that kind of direct, data-first conversation is a big part of why.

**4. What you enjoy most about working with people and helping businesses succeed**

The part I like most is the moment an owner stops seeing me as a vendor and starts treating me as part of how they run the business. That's when the real work happens, when they trust the recommendation because they've seen it backed up by their own numbers. I've built my whole career around that shift, from managing SaaS accounts to running my own company now, and it's still the most satisfying part of the job.

**5. Why you'd be a great fit**

SanterMedia's model, and the coaching seat specifically, is close to what I already do every day. I manage a portfolio of business owners, I read their numbers, and I tell them the truth even when it's not what they want to hear, because that's what actually moves their results. I don't have fitness-industry history, but I have years of doing this exact job, lead flow, accountability, retention, for owners who are good at their craft and need someone managing the business side with them. I think that makes me a strong fit for this role, and I'd like the chance to prove it.
