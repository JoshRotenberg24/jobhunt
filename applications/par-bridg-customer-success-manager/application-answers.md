# PAR Technology (Bridg) — Application Answers

## Why do you want to work at PAR?

Restaurants are where I started. I cooked for over 15 years before moving into software, so I've seen how much a restaurant runs on knowing its regulars, and how little most operators actually know about the guests who don't join a loyalty program. Bridg closes that gap with data the restaurant already has. That's a problem I understand from the kitchen side.

I've also sold a loyalty platform (Fivestars) and managed partners on a data-aggregator platform at Birdeye, so the product makes sense to me from day one. What I want next is to go deeper on the data side, identity resolution and retail media, and do it in a customer success role where I'm helping existing clients get more value, which is the work I've liked most in every job. PAR is one of the few places where all three of those meet.
