# Cuvo Health, Client Success Manager: brief statement

## A client implementation or account you managed and the outcome

At Birdeye I owned onboarding and the ongoing relationship for 15 to 20 reseller partners, agencies that resold our reputation and listings platform to their own clients, including several focused on healthcare. Each partner launch had the same moving parts: configuring the platform for the partner, setting up our listings-syndication solution correctly for every one of their end customers, and training their team well enough to sell and support it themselves. I ran those launches concurrently, built automated training sequences so every partner got the same onboarding, and tracked status in Salesforce. When something broke, I brought clear, prioritized issues to Product and Engineering and kept the partner informed until it was fixed. The outcome: I retained all but one partner during my tenure, roughly 94%, on deals of about $10K to $45K each.
