# Fleetio Senior Lifecycle Marketing Manager: Application Answers

## Throughout your experience as a lifecycle marketer, what parts of the customer journey have you owned?

At Solenzo I own the full journey end to end. I built a 20+ workflow system in GoHighLevel covering lead capture, tagging and routing, scoring and qualification, booking with confirmations and reminders, no-show recovery, post-sale onboarding nurture, long-term nurture and reactivation, and review generation, across email and SMS. I also set the lifecycle stage definitions and lead scoring that decide which journey a contact enters.

Before that I owned the post-sale stages hands-on. At Wix I onboarded 25+ clients a month and drove them to launch, and at Birdeye I owned onboarding, adoption, and retention for 15 to 20 reseller partners, built automated training sequences, and retained all but one. That gives me a practical view of activation and retention that I now build into the automated side.

## From your experience in B2B SaaS, what type of go-to-market motion did the company have?

Mostly sales-assisted, with a few different shapes. Accelo was sales-led to agencies and professional services firms, with demos, scoping, and a handoff to implementation. Birdeye ran a channel motion, selling and onboarding through reseller partners alongside direct sales. At Wix, a largely self-serve platform, I worked in a sales-assisted onboarding vertical for clients who wanted help launching. At Solenzo I run a hybrid: a free digital-footprint audit as a product-led entry point (180+ businesses enrolled), followed by automated nurture and a sales conversation.

## What marketing automation platforms have you used most extensively?

GoHighLevel is my primary platform. I've built and run full lifecycle automation in it since 2024, including scoring, routing, nurture, and reactivation across email and SMS. I've also built in HubSpot for clients and in agency roles, worked in Salesforce at Birdeye, Wix, and Fivestars, and run email in Mailchimp. For integrations and custom logic I use Zapier, Make, and n8n with webhooks and API calls. I evaluated Marketo at Fetch & Funnel but haven't administered it day to day.
