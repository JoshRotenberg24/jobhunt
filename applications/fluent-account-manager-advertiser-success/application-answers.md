# Fluent: Application Question Answers

## Tell us about a time when something was going wrong with an advertiser's campaign.

**The issue:** A service-business client was running Google Ads and getting lead volume, but a large share of those leads were spam and junk. The owner was paying for clicks and losing time chasing contacts that were never going to book.

**How I found it:** Every lead came in through the client's website, so I started there instead of assuming the ad account was the problem. That's where it was breaking down.

**What I solved on my own:** I cleaned up the website, and the junk leads stopped. No extra spend and no campaign rebuild.

**What I brought to the client:** Once the leads were real, the next gap showed up. Good traffic was landing but not converting at the rate it should. I brought that to the owner as a clear recommendation: the ads were doing their job, and the next gain was in on-site conversion, not more budget.

The lesson I carry into every account: follow the lead all the way to revenue before deciding what's broken. The fix isn't always inside the ad platform.
