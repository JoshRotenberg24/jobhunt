# Application Answers — AI Success Manager, Cresta

## Walk us through a time you helped an organization change how they work.

At Wix, I helped build a brand-new account vertical from an unproven concept into a
profitable line of business, and I did the process work underneath that shift, not just
the client work attached to it.

The vertical didn't have a repeatable onboarding motion when I joined it. I built one,
then packaged what worked into training I delivered to new hires and to tenured account
executives, since a team that new needed everyone operating the same way, not just the
newest people on it. I also led hiring and onboarding as the team scaled from a handful of
people up to 20, then settled at a core team of 8 once we understood the right shape for
the work.

What actually moved: the vertical went from proof of concept to a profitable, impactful
business line. I personally onboarded 25+ clients a month while holding 120% of sales and
110% of implementation targets through that whole stretch, and I was the one who kept
pitching the process changes that let the team stay efficient as headcount shifted
underneath it. That's the part I'd point to as my role, not managing what existed,
building what didn't.

## Strategy consultant, AI product expert, or account owner: which is home, which is the stretch?

Account owner is home. Every role I've had, whether I was carrying a quota or running my
own company, has centered on inheriting a relationship and being the person who keeps it
healthy, expands it, and earns the renewal. That's true at Birdeye, where I retained 94%
of a 15-20 partner book, and it's true at Solenzo today.

AI product expert is the stretch. Not because the skill isn't real, it's current and
hands-on, I architect and deploy agent systems for clients now, but because it's the
newest part of what I do. I've been doing account ownership and consultative selling for
15 years. I've been building AI systems for about two and a half. Getting to the same
depth on Cresta's specific platform that I have on my own stack is the real ramp, and I'd
rather say that directly than pretend otherwise.
