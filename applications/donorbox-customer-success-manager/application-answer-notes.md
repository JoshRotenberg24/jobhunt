## 1. Project with no clear directions, only a goal

At Wix I was part of a brand-new vertical that had a goal, make it a real business line, and no playbook for how to get there. I led onboarding teams while the company tested different team structures, anywhere from 8 people up to about 30, and I was the first to pitch process changes and lead team initiatives as we figured out what worked. I helped hire and train new specialists and built sales training for tenured account executives, while holding 120% of my sales targets and 110% of implementation targets. The vertical went from proof of concept to a profitable business line. What I learned was [your answer]. What I'd do better is [your answer].

## 2. Great accomplishment (3-4 sentences)

At Birdeye I managed a book of 15 to 20 reseller partners with deals from $10K to $45K, and I retained all but one of them. I started by watching partner usage data to see who was falling behind on adoption. Then I built automated training sequences so onboarding stopped stalling, and coached partners on customer health metrics so they could show their own clients the value. That combination is what kept the book intact.

## 3. What do you think about AI?

I use AI every day and build with it for my clients, mostly to handle the repetitive layer: sorting leads, drafting first versions, filling in data. It's good at that, and it frees up time for the part customers actually remember, which is a person who notices they're struggling and helps. I also understand why you want applications in candidates' own words, since the point is to hear how someone actually thinks.

## 4. What is your working style?

I've worked remotely since 2020 and run my own practice, so I'm used to setting my own priorities and following through without someone checking in. I reach out before customers have to ask, and I raise problems early rather than hoping they resolve. I document everything in the CRM so anyone on the team can pick up an account. And I write in plain language, because most of the people I help aren't technical.

## 5. Why are you the ideal candidate?

This role is about being the first person a new customer talks to and getting them to value quickly, and that's work I've done at volume: at Wix I onboarded 25+ small businesses a month and aimed to launch each one on the first call. I've also built the education side, publishing 18 plain-language articles for non-technical owners and running workshops and training, and I ran a free-offer program for my own business, so I know how a free user decides to pay. I have more experience than the posting asks for, and I'm applying because I want this work for organizations whose missions I care about, especially those serving immigrant and refugee communities.
