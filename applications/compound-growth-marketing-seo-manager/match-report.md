## Top strengths for this role

1. **The AI-tool and AI-search fit is close to exact.** The posting wants someone who
   "already treats AI visibility as essential SEO work" and names Claude Code, ChatGPT
   Codex, and Google AI Studio directly. Daily hands-on use of all three, plus a
   rubric-based grading system built for AI output, is a genuine, specific match — not a
   generalist "AI-curious" claim.
2. **Verifying evidence instead of trusting a dashboard.** The BTU Solar conversion-
   tracking root-cause (two stacked, non-obvious bugs, verified via direct browser
   inspection) is exactly the discipline the posting asks for under AEO: "bring hard
   evidence... what changed, what moved, and why," in a space with no standardized
   tooling yet.
3. **Real people-management experience.** Two years managing and developing a team
   (20, then 8) at Wix, including hiring and training, directly supports the "working
   manager" / team-leadership half of the role, which many SEO-only candidates won't have.
