# Workyard, Scaled Customer Success Manager: Application Answers

## In a few words, outline your experience with owning renewals and retention for SMB/Mid-Market customers:

At Birdeye I owned retention for a book of 15-20 reseller partners (deals ~$10K-$45K) serving SMB end customers, and kept all but one (~94%) by tracking utilization in Salesforce and stepping in on adoption gaps before they turned into churn. At Wix I onboarded and managed 25+ small-business accounts a month for two years at 110% of implementation targets. Today I own client retention and retainer expansion for my own consulting book of HVAC, electrical, and plumbing contractors.

## Additional Comments

My clients right now are trade contractors, so I know the owner is on a job site, and a straight answer by phone beats a deck. I'd approach the first dedicated retention role by testing the health score against the accounts that actually churned, then building the program around what that shows.
