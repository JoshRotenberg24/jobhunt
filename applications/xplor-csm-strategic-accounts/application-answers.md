## Industry challenge question

Boutique fitness lives or dies on retention. A studio can fill a first class with promotions, but members drift away when nothing follows up, and owners of multi-location brands rarely have time to dig into the booking and attendance data that shows who is slipping. The software already holds those signals. Most operators don't have a system that acts on them.

That's the problem I've spent years solving for small businesses. At Solenzo I built automated booking confirmations, reminders, no-show recovery, and reactivation of lapsed customers for service businesses, so follow up happens without the owner chasing it. At Fivestars I sold a loyalty platform to local businesses trying to bring customers back, and at Birdeye I watched partner usage closely and retained all but one of 15 to 20 accounts. For a strategic fitness account, I'd help leadership turn the data already in Xplor into a repeatable retention play across every location.
