How do you typically manage urgent requests while still keeping tracking organized and accurate?
I confirm exactly what needs to change and why before I touch the container, since most urgent requests are smaller than they first sound. I make the change, test it in GTM Preview and the browser's network tab and dataLayer, and only publish once I've seen the event fire correctly. I publish with a clear version name and a short note on what changed, then log it in the project tool so the next person knows what's live. Fast doesn't have to mean messy if the test and the note are part of the habit, not an extra step.
