# Revenue Enablement Platform Program Manager — NetApp

- **Locations (hybrid, in-office expectations TBD):** Morrisville, NC; Wichita, KS;
  San Jose, CA; New York, NY; Boulder, CO; Vienna, VA; Waltham, MA
- **Compensation:** $170,000–$253,000 target salary range (location/experience-dependent)
- **Job category:** Amer Sales · Job ID: 135483-en_US

Provides dedicated execution leadership across Revenue Enablement strategic programs
(The Sales Hub, Mindtickle, Saba transition, operating-model workstreams). Translates
strategic direction into executable plans; keeps work moving by clarifying scope,
aligning stakeholders, tracking progress, surfacing issues early, documenting decisions.

**Primary responsibilities:** execution planning across platform initiatives; integrated
project plans/timelines/action registers/dependency trackers/decision logs; cross-
functional stakeholder coordination (Revenue Enablement, IT, ops, content owners,
platform admins, activation teams, dev teams, reporting, leadership sponsors); translate
priorities into workstreams/deliverables/owners/timelines; facilitate working sessions
and status meetings; identify/escalate risks and blockers with recommended options;
support process/governance documentation; prepare leadership readouts and decision
materials; track commitments/follow-through; support change management/adoption
planning; improve team operating model.

**Required:** strong program/project management (planning, prioritization, dependency
and risk management, stakeholder coordination); turning ambiguity into structured plans;
organizational discipline across multiple workstreams; strong written/verbal
communication to senior leaders; proactive ownership; comfort in fast-paced
transformation environment; influence without direct authority; attention to detail and
follow-through.

**Preferred:** experience supporting digital platforms, sales/revenue enablement,
learning platforms, or enterprise transformation programs; familiarity with MS Office,
Teams, SharePoint, project tracking tools, dashboard/reporting inputs.
