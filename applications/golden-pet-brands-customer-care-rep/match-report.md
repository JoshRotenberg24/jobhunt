## The real story here: overqualification, not a skills gap

Every stated minimum (HS diploma/GED, 1-2 years customer service) is trivially exceeded
by 15+ years of experience. That's not the issue. The posting labels itself "Junior" and
describes a queue-based contact-center role: handling cancellations, order status,
shipping questions, retention offers, and standard contact-center performance metrics.
That's a different job than anything in the profile, individual-contributor,
transactional, metrics-driven ticket handling, not strategic account ownership, CRM
architecture, or team leadership. No eligibility failures, but a real scope mismatch in
the other direction.
