## What do you look for when deciding whether an inbound lead is qualified to pass to an AE? (350 chars)

Fit first: right role, company size, and a real use case for the product. Then need: what made them sign up or ask for a demo, what they use today, and what isn't working. Then timing and who decides. If they fit, have a real problem, and can act in a reasonable window, they go to the AE with notes so nobody re-asks the basics.

## A time you followed up multiple times before connecting. How did you stay persistent without hurting the experience?

With my consulting business, most businesses that took my free website audit didn't reply the first time. Instead of sending the same "just checking in" note, I made every follow-up add something: a specific finding from their audit, a quick fix they could make that day, or a short example from a similar business. I spaced touches out and changed the channel between email and LinkedIn. Prospects who replied usually said the audit details were why. More than 180 businesses went through that audit, and two became paying clients.
