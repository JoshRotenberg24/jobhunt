## What is Demand Response?
Demand response is when energy users reduce or shift their electricity use during periods of high demand or grid stress, usually in exchange for payment. Grid operators and utilities call "events" when supply is tight, and participating sites curtail load by adjusting HVAC, pausing equipment, or running on backup power. It's cheaper and cleaner than firing up extra power plants, and it lets businesses earn money from flexibility they already have. Companies like Voltus handle the market registration, dispatch signals, and performance measurement so the site can participate without managing that complexity.
