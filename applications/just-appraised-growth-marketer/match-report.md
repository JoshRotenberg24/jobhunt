## Top strengths for this role

1. **He builds channels that don't exist yet.** The posting is explicit that email is a
   greenfield build. Solenzo's automation system was built from nothing, solo — the
   closest possible analogue.
2. **Customer marketing sits on real CS and sales experience.** Most growth marketers
   partner with CS from the outside. Josh has been the CS-side person, which is what
   makes expansion plays land instead of stalling in a handoff.
3. **Full-stack without a team.** Copy, landing pages, automation, segmentation,
   reporting, and enablement content are all things he has personally shipped.
4. **AI and automation practice** — relevant twice over at a company whose product is
   NLP/AI, and as leverage for a one-person function.

  government procurement cycles or the county-assessor buyer.
