## Honest answers for the real gaps

Don't dodge these if asked. A confident, honest answer beats a stretched one.

**Payroll systems, timekeeping software, HRIS:**
> "I haven't used a dedicated payroll or timekeeping system specifically, but I've adopted new software fast throughout my career, CRM platforms, automation tools, reporting systems, and I'd expect to be productive quickly on whatever Sprouts uses."

**Bookkeeping/accounting software:**
> "That's not something I've done directly. What I have done is keep financial and operational records accurate under real accountability, cash handling at Standing Akimbo, budget oversight at Wix, and I'd bring that same discipline to learning your specific systems."

**Microsoft Excel/Outlook/Word:**
> "My day-to-day tools have been Google Sheets and Docs rather than the Microsoft suite specifically, but they do the same job, and I pick up new software quickly. That's not going to be the thing that slows me down."

Do **not** claim Excel proficiency you don't have, if they test it or ask a specific formula/function question, an honest "I'm more fluent in Sheets but the concepts transfer" holds up better than a claim that falls apart under a follow-up.

**Grocery/retail-store industry experience:**
> "I don't have grocery-specific experience. What I bring instead is real retail-operations experience from Standing Akimbo, cash handling, compliance, customer and vendor systems, plus fifteen years of the organizational and people-facing discipline this role needs day to day."
