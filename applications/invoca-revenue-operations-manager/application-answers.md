voice journeys off trustworthy first-party data, is close to what I already build for my
own clients at Solenzo. I've architected a 20+-workflow automation system that captures,

proven version of exactly that.

Sales, Marketing, and CS ops split into silos, also matches how I actually operate. At
Solenzo I own the full lifecycle myself: discovery, CRM architecture, lead flow, and
reporting, one person accountable for the whole system rather than a single department's
slice of it. That's closer to how this role is structured than a traditional RevOps job
split by function.

I also come in with hands-on experience across the specific stack this role touches,
Salesforce, Salesloft, ZoomInfo, and Gong, plus Zapier, Make, and n8n on the automation
layer, so the ramp here is shorter than it would be for someone learning the tools on the
job.
