## What factors do you consider most important when considering a new role/company?

Three things. First, ownership of a real outcome. I do my best work when I own the customer from setup through adoption, not just one handoff in the middle. Second, a product customers actually depend on, where getting the details right before go-live matters. Third, a team that lets me fix things. When I see the same blocker three times, I want to be able to document it and build the automation that removes it.

## What AI specific tools are you currently leveraging? In what use cases have you found AI beneficial? How has AI influenced how you approach your day to day?

I use ChatGPT and Claude daily, and I call the OpenAI and Claude APIs directly inside production workflows I've built in n8n, Make, and Zapier.

Where it's paid off most:
- **Client automation.** I built workflows that score a business's digital footprint and generate personalized outreach from it. One audit workflow enrolled 180+ businesses.
- **Quality control.** I wrote rubric-based grading criteria for my SEO audit process and use them to evaluate AI-generated output against a defined standard, labeling the data myself.
- **Debugging.** When a webhook or API call breaks, I use AI to read the JSON payload and error with me, which gets me to the fix much faster than searching docs.
- **Translating requests.** Clients usually describe what they want loosely. I turn that into precise prompts and agent instructions so the output is reliable.

The biggest change in my day to day is that I build first. Instead of writing a request and waiting on someone else, I prototype the fix, test it, and hand over something working.
