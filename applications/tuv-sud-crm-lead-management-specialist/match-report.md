## Top strengths for this role

1. **The task list is his build list.** Lead routing, scoring, segmentation, nurture
   automation, data hygiene, dashboards — the Solenzo system covers every one by name, and
   he built it rather than inheriting it.
2. **CRM-based AI agents.** The posting's last task bullet is the hardest one for most
   applicants and the easiest one for him. Worth leading with in any interview.
3. **Genuine multi-platform depth.** Salesforce, HubSpot, and GoHighLevel hands-on, plus
   having sold and scoped CRM systems to other companies at Accelo — he's seen a lot of
   other organizations' lead processes from the inside.
4. **Both sides of the lead handoff.** A decade carrying a bag before building the systems.
   He knows how a routing rule fails in practice, which is the thing this role exists to fix.
