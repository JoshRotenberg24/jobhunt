# Hummingbirds, Performance Marketer: employer questions

## Please share details about a marketing program you've built that you are most proud of.

The program I'm proudest of is the acquisition engine I built for my own business, Solenzo, because I owned every piece of it. The offer was a free digital-footprint audit for local service businesses. I built an AI-assisted workflow in GoHighLevel that scored each business's online presence (website visibility, reviews, missed-call handling) and used the results to trigger personalized outreach, then split follow-up into segmented email campaigns by the weakness each audit found. I drove top-of-funnel demand through my own LinkedIn content rather than paid spend.

180+ businesses enrolled in the audit, and 2 became paying clients. That conversion rate is early and I don't oversell it, but the program taught me the discipline I'd bring to Hummingbirds: define the offer, instrument every step so you know where people drop, segment by what the data actually says, and let results settle the argument. The same system now feeds a 20+ workflow SMS and email nurture and booking engine, so leads move straight from first touch into follow-up.
