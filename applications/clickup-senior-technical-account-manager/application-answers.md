## Why did you decide to apply to this role at ClickUp?

This role combines the two things I do best: owning customer relationships and rebuilding how work gets done with automation and AI. In my consulting practice I take clients' manual workflows apart and rebuild them as automated systems and AI agents, and I've run client work in ClickUp myself, so I've seen how much a well-built workspace changes a team's day. ClickUp putting AI agents at the center of the product, and asking its account managers to design and deploy them with customers, is exactly the kind of work I want to be doing.
