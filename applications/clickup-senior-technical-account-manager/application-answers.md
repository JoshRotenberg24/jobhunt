# ClickUp, Senior Technical Account Manager: Application Answers

## What is your desired salary range for this role, including base and any variable or on-target earnings?

$120,000 to $135,000 base. I'm open on the variable component and happy to discuss total on-target earnings based on how the plan is structured.

## Why did you decide to apply to this role at ClickUp?

This role combines the two things I do best: owning customer relationships and rebuilding how work gets done with automation and AI. In my consulting practice I take clients' manual workflows apart and rebuild them as automated systems and AI agents, and I've run client work in ClickUp myself, so I've seen how much a well-built workspace changes a team's day. ClickUp putting AI agents at the center of the product, and asking its account managers to design and deploy them with customers, is exactly the kind of work I want to be doing.

## Please describe the Customer Portfolios you've managed (ARR, Number of Customers, etc.)

At Birdeye I managed a portfolio of 15 to 20 reseller partners, with individual deals ranging from about $10K to $45K, across health, home services, and retail. At Wix I managed a high-value portfolio of small and mid-size business accounts and personally onboarded 25+ new clients a month for two years, while leading a team of 20 (later 8) onboarding specialists. At Accelo I closed and implemented deals of $20K and up with agencies and professional-services firms, averaging about $17,750 a month in services bookings (about $213K annualized) plus new recurring revenue. Today I run up to three concurrent client engagements at my own consulting practice.

## What impact do you have on ARR? Help us understand if you've managed renewal contracts, upsells, cross-sells, etc.

My biggest ARR impact has come from retention and expansion. At Birdeye I kept all but one of my partners, about 94%, by tracking utilization and stepping in before adoption gaps turned into churn. In my consulting practice I grow existing accounts by identifying and scoping complementary services that expand retainers. On the new-revenue side, I hit 120% of sales targets at Wix, ranked top three at Accelo while quota roughly doubled, and closed about $130K in new bookings at Fetch & Funnel.

## How do you use AI as a Customer Success Manager?

I use AI the way I'd want customers to: to take repetitive work off the table and make the human part sharper. In my practice I break clients' manual workflows into AI agents, write the prompts and logic, and grade the output against rubrics I build before anyone relies on it. The same habits apply to customer success: turning a customer's loosely described goals into a precise workflow, drafting enablement and follow-up at scale, and building agents that handle routine steps so check-ins can focus on outcomes. The discipline I bring is verification. I test AI output against the real system and a clear standard before it reaches a customer.
