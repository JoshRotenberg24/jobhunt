# AutoVitals — Application Answers

## 1. This role is answering the same 20 questions most of the day, while staying fast, accurate and friendly. How would you feel about that type of work, and what would help you stay engaged and consistent?

I'd be comfortable with it. I cooked in restaurants for over 15 years, and a line is the same 20 dishes every night. The job is making the 200th plate as good as the first, because it's the first one that guest has ever had. Support works the same way. The question repeats, but the shop owner asking it is having a different day.

What keeps me consistent is having a system: saved replies and clean notes for the common questions, so speed never costs accuracy. What keeps me engaged is noticing patterns. If the same question keeps coming in, that usually means a help article is unclear or a setting is hard to find, and flagging that is a real way to make the next day easier for everyone. At Wix I onboarded 25+ clients a month through the same core steps, and that's the rhythm I did best in.
