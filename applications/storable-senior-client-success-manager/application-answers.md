# Storable, Senior Client Success Manager: Application Answers

## Tell us about a piece of work or accomplishment you are proud of that connects to this role. What did you personally do, and what was the outcome?

At Birdeye I owned a book of 15 to 20 reseller partners, agencies serving local businesses in health, home services, and retail, with deals from about $10K to $45K. I onboarded each partner, guided their teams through the technical setup of Birdeye's listings and reputation tools, and built automated training sequences so their staff could get up to speed without waiting on me. After launch I tracked each partner's usage in Salesforce, and when adoption dropped I stepped in early with a specific recommendation, whether that was retraining a team, changing a workflow, or showing them how to present the product's value to their own clients. When partners hit problems the product couldn't solve, I brought structured feedback to Product and Engineering. I kept all but one partner, about 94% retention. That's the work I'd bring to Storable's key clients: understanding how an operator actually runs their business, getting the configuration right, and catching risk before it turns into churn.
