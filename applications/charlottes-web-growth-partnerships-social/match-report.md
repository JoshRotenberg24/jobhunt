1. **The partner program analogy is real, not stretched.** Recruit, onboard, activate,
   retain — Birdeye was exactly that motion, with a number attached (~94%) and the
   enablement materials to show for it.
2. **Regulated industry.** Charlotte's Web sells a product where marketing claims are
   legally constrained. A growth marketer who has built compliance systems from scratch and
   treats review as part of the work rather than an obstacle is genuinely uncommon.
3. **He writes it himself.** The storytelling and content-leadership section asks for
   someone who can translate complex concepts into audience narratives. That's documented
   across thought leadership, guides, and copy.
4. **Local.** Twenty minutes to Louisville, no relocation.
