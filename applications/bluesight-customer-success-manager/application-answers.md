# Application Answers — Customer Success Manager, Bluesight

## Why Bluesight?

Bluesight's mission, getting the right medicine to the right patient at the right time,
resonates with me personally. I've spent roughly eight years navigating the healthcare
system myself as a patient, fighting insurers for approvals on physical therapy and
imaging, so I understand firsthand what it costs when the right care doesn't reach the
right person on schedule. Professionally, a meaningful share of the partner book I
managed at Birdeye were agencies serving healthcare clients, real B2B exposure to how
healthcare organizations buy and adopt technology.

I bring 15 years of customer success and account management experience: at Birdeye, I
managed a reseller partner book and retained 94% of it by staying close to customer
health metrics and intervening on adoption gaps before they became churn. At Accelo, I
built trusted advisor relationships directly with C-suite executives, guiding onboarding
and escalating risk early enough to resolve it before it threatened the relationship.
