## Q1. "Please share your experience with digital marketing for a consumer subscription app."

**Status: ready to paste (~1,150 characters).**

I haven't run acquisition for a consumer subscription app, and I'd rather say that plainly
than stretch it. My growth work has been B2B and service-business.

What transfers is the mechanics. At Solenzo I built a 20+ workflow acquisition and
lifecycle system covering capture, routing, qualification, booking with confirmations and
reminders, no-show recovery, onboarding nurture, and long-term reactivation. That's the
same shape as a subscription funnel: bring them in, activate them quickly, and re-engage
them before they lapse. At Level Agency I built the reporting frameworks tracking ROI,
CAC, LTV, and conversion KPIs, and used them to make budget recommendations. On one paid
account I cut the spam out of targeting, then followed the remaining conversion gap past
the ad platform into the on-site experience, which is where the problem actually was.

The closest I've come to subscription context is Wix, itself a subscription product, where
I managed an e-commerce portfolio that contributed to a 30% increase in traffic and a 25%
improvement in conversion rates across the segment. That was account and onboarding work
rather than acquisition, so I'd call it adjacent rather than equivalent.
