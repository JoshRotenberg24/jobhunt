The client is an established digital marketing agency with a 17-year track record. The
Senior Account Manager owns client relationships directly — trusted advisor connecting
marketing performance to business outcomes across SEO, AEO, Paid Media, CRO, and
Analytics. High-visibility, direct access to leadership; grows into building/leading the
account management function.

**Key responsibilities:** own senior client relationships with VP/C-level stakeholders;
lead structured client meetings and QBRs; translate technical channel work into business
narratives; coordinate execution across SEO/CRO/paid media/analytics/content; maintain
organized project environments (ClickUp or similar); facilitate goal-setting/KPI
alignment; protect and grow accounts (churn risk, upsell, renewals).

**Required:** 4+ yrs Account Management/Client Strategy/Client Success within a digital
marketing agency; enterprise or mid-market eCommerce and/or B2B portfolio experience;
technical fluency (not execution) in SEO/paid media/CRO/analytics; VP/C-level
relationship-building; strong PM instincts; excellent written/verbal communication;
retention/growth/satisfaction track record.

**Preferred:** eCommerce platforms (Shopify/Magento/BigCommerce); AEO/GEO familiarity;
experience building/scaling an AM function; GA4/Looker Studio comfort.
