## What area(s) of digital marketing do you have the most experience in?

Client and account strategy is the strongest thread across my career, running strategic
relationships and full-lifecycle programs for B2B and e-commerce clients at Solenzo,
Level Agency, Birdeye, and Wix. Inside that, I have the deepest hands-on depth in CRM
and marketing-automation architecture (GoHighLevel primarily, HubSpot for client builds)
and in the reporting layer that connects channel work to business outcomes, ROI, CAC,
LTV, and conversion tracking through GA4 and campaign attribution. I also run technical
SEO audits and Google Ads optimization directly, which is what lets me translate what
SEO, CRO, and paid-media specialists are doing into a narrative a non-technical
executive can act on, rather than just relaying their reports.

## Briefly describe a time you retained, expanded, or turned around a client account. What actions did you take, and what was the outcome?

At Birdeye I owned the relationship for 15 to 20 reseller partners, deal sizes roughly
$10K to $45K, and inherited a partner book where adoption varied widely. Rather than
waiting for a partner to flag a problem, I tracked utilization and product-adoption data
against each partner's history to catch the accounts that were quietly falling behind
before they became churn risks. Where a partner was underusing the platform, I advised
them directly on retention strategy and customer-health metrics, and worked with them to
adopt automated communication and training workflows so their own onboarding got faster
and less dependent on manual follow-up. I also fed structured, high-priority feedback
from those conversations back to Product and Engineering, since some of what looked like
an adoption problem was actually a product gap worth escalating. Over that tenure I
retained all but one partner in the book, roughly 94% retention, and the automated
workflows I put in place outlasted my day-to-day involvement with any single account.
