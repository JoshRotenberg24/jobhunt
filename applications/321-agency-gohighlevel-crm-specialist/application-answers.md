## A workflow built from scratch

I built an automated audit workflow for my own practice. The trigger was a new contact entering the pipeline from outreach. The workflow scored the business's digital footprint (website visibility, reviews, missed-call handling), tagged the contact by weakness, and sent personalized outreach based on that score, with follow-up steps that stopped once someone replied or booked. It enrolled 180+ businesses and converted two into paying clients. The bigger result was the template: the same scoring and tagging logic now drives segmented campaigns I build for clients.

## Setting up a client with a snapshot

I'd start with discovery so I know what has to change before I load anything: their services, sales process, team roles, and lead sources. After loading the snapshot, I'd customize custom values (business name, phone, links, hours), pipeline stages to match their actual sales process, calendars and availability, user roles and permissions, email and SMS copy in their voice, and tags and naming conventions to our standard. Before going live I'd run every workflow end to end with a test contact, check every link and personalization field, confirm the sending domain and SMS registration are approved, check form and booking submissions land in the right pipeline stage, and confirm notifications reach the right people. Then I'd train the team and document what's custom to that client.

## SMS and email compliance and deliverability

On email, I set up the sending domain myself, including SPF, DKIM, and DMARC, warm up new domains before volume goes out, and keep lists clean, which has held deliverability above 99%. On SMS, I treat A2P 10DLC brand and campaign registration as a prerequisite: nothing sends until it's approved, and every message carries clear opt-out language. I only message contacts with documented consent, honor STOP and unsubscribe requests immediately, and keep suppression lists current. I've also set up consent configuration on client websites so opt-in is captured correctly at the source.
