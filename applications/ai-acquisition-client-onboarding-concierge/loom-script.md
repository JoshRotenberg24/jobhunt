# Loom Script — Jamie's Live Welcome Call (target 5:00–5:30, hard cap 6:00)

Talking points, not a word-for-word script. Read through twice, then record off the beats below in your own phrasing. Sounding natural matters more than hitting every line.

## Setup (0:00–0:10)

One sentence to camera before going into character, so the reviewer knows what they're watching:

> "This is Jamie's Welcome Call, about 15 minutes after signing up for Starter."

Then go straight into the roleplay. Don't break again until the call ends.

## Beat 1: Warm open, build confidence (0:10–1:00)

- Greet Jamie by name, congratulate him on getting started.
- Set expectations for the call up front: "This'll take about ten minutes, and by the end you're fully set up."
- Address the nerves directly and normally, don't over-reassure: "If tech's not your thing, no stress, that's exactly what I'm here for."

## Beat 2: Solve the login live (1:00–2:15)

- Walk Jamie through finding the email in real time: "Pull up your inbox, search 'Starter', check spam if it's not in the main folder."
- Get him to click through and land on the platform.
- Have him bookmark it on the spot so this never comes up again.
- Keep it collaborative, not instructional: ask what he's seeing at each step rather than narrating at him.

## Beat 3: Explain what happens next, in plain language (2:15–3:15)

- Two calls total: this one, then a longer Onboarding Call later.
- Before the Welcome Call finishes today: a short questionnaire on the platform, a few minutes, just so the team knows where he's starting from.
- Before the Onboarding Call: three short exercises (Audience, Problem, Solution) — mention them by name so he's not surprised later.

## Beat 4: Move him to action (3:15–4:15)

- Do the questionnaire together, right now, while you're on the phone: "Let's knock this out together so it's one less thing on your plate."
- Offer two concrete times for the Welcome Call booking (or confirm it's this same call) and get a yes.
- Close warmly: recap what got done (logged in, questionnaire complete, next step booked) and tell him exactly how to reach you before then.

## Beat 5: Break character, explain the no-answer path (4:15–5:30)

Step out of the roleplay and speak to camera directly:

- "If Jamie hadn't picked up, here's what happens instead."
- Send SMS + email immediately with the Welcome Call booking link, per the 30-minute rule.
- Include the login help in that same email, since Jamie already flagged it as the blocker.
- Log the attempt: time, channel, no answer, link sent, login resent.
- Set a follow-up call for the same time tomorrow, built into your task list rather than left to memory.

## Delivery notes

- Stay in character fully through Beats 1–4. Don't narrate the rules while roleplaying, that's what Beat 5 is for.
- If you run long, trim Beat 2 (the login walk-through) rather than Beat 5 (the no-answer protocol) — the protocol explanation is what's actually being graded in the back half.
- Record in one take. If a section goes sideways, keep going rather than restarting, since the brief calls for unedited.
