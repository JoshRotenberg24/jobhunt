## The honest headline

**One required line is a hard miss and it's the first one: "5–8 years of product marketing
experience in B2B SaaS, with demonstrated ownership of positioning, launches, and sales
enablement."** Josh has never held the PMM title. No amount of tailoring changes that, and
many teams screen on it directly.

What the full posting revealed, though, is that the *rest* of the req suits him better than
the generic PMM shape did. Two things in particular:

1. **He is the category.** Seamless sells AI-powered contact data and prospecting. Josh
   builds AI-driven prospecting systems — contact enrichment, digital-footprint scoring,
   automated outreach — as his current job. He is a practitioner-buyer in this exact market.
2. **The preferred list names his daily tools.** "Proficiency with AI tools to accelerate
   GTM workflows (e.g., Claude, ChatGPT...)" is a listed preference and is literally what he
   does all day. Most PMM applicants will claim familiarity; he ships with them.

## Top strengths for this role

1. **Category insider.** He builds AI prospecting and contact-enrichment systems for a
   living. He knows where tools in this space actually disappoint a rep, which is exactly
   the insight competitive positioning runs on.
2. **AI GTM tooling is a named preference and his default mode.** Claude and ChatGPT in
   production, agent design, prompt engineering.
3. **He has been the persona.** The posting wants messaging that lands with Sales,
   Marketing, and RevOps buyers. Ten years carrying a bag, top three of 8–15 reps at Accelo
   while quota doubled, thousands of live objection and competitor conversations.

category, and you've been the buyer their messaging has to move. That's a credible pitch to
