# EdSights — Application Answers

## Anything else you'd like us to know?

My path runs from sales into implementation and systems work, and the thread through all of it is the part I like best: taking a customer who has already said yes and making the product actually work for them. That's why I'm looking at implementation roles specifically. Education also runs deep in my family, so helping colleges keep students enrolled is a mission I'd be glad to put my work behind.

## Describe a complex implementation you've led. What made it complex, and what was your personal role? (3-5 sentences)

At Accelo, most incoming clients were professional-services firms replacing several systems at once (CRM, project management, ticketing, and billing), each with its own data and team habits. The complex ones followed the same shape: the risk sat in deciding what to change first, because switching everything at once broke the workflows people relied on to get paid. My role was to scope each implementation during the sale and build a sequenced plan that ranked which systems to move and in what order, then hand it to the delivery team to execute. I also ran workshops with each client team showing how their part of the platform would change their daily work, which kept adoption moving while the rollout happened in stages.

## Describe a time a partner changed their approach or timeline because of your recommendation. (3-5 sentences)

At Wix I aimed to get every client live on the first call, but some clients came in wanting a full e-commerce store launched immediately when their products, photos, and content weren't ready. In those cases I recommended slowing down and planning the site production across several sessions instead of launching something half-finished. They trusted it because I'd already walked them through exactly what was missing and what a weak launch would cost them in traffic and sales, rather than just telling them to wait. Those launches took longer, but they went live with a store that worked.
