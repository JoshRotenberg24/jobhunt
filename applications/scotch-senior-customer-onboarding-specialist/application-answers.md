# Scotch Senior Customer Onboarding Specialist: Application Answers

## Example of leading a customer through a technical implementation or onboarding

At Solenzo I run GoHighLevel implementations for home-services businesses, and they follow the same shape. I start with discovery to map how the business actually moves a lead from first call to booked job, then configure the system to match: pipeline stages, contact fields, lead routing, booking calendars, and automated confirmations and reminders by email and SMS. Google Business Profile setup goes first because everything else builds on it.

The hiccups usually show up in the data and the integrations. I import the client's existing contacts and re-check the records after import instead of trusting the upload. When a webhook or integration fails, I debug it myself by reading the payload and finding where the handoff broke, so the client never has to chase me for an answer.

For confidence, I train the owner and staff hands-on in their own account and document what's live and what happens next. At Wix I ran this same process at volume, onboarding 25+ small businesses a month with about 25 in flight at once. I aimed to launch each one on the first call and planned multi-session builds for complex retailers so go-live never slipped.
