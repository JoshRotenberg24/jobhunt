## Why are you interested in this opportunity with Actabl?

I spent more than 15 years cooking in restaurants before moving into SaaS, so software built for hospitality operators is personal to me. I know how hard those teams work and how quickly a tool either earns its place or gets ignored. This role is also the work I'm best at: owning an existing customer relationship, learning what the business is trying to accomplish, and using usage data to grow adoption before risk shows up. At Birdeye that approach kept all but one of my 15 to 20 partners. Being able to do that for the service industry, out of your Denver hub, is a rare fit.
