## Eligibility cap applied — 25/100

Both qualification paths on this posting share one non-optional clause: **"which must
include at least 2 years as a performance analyst developing key performance indicators
(KPIs) in a federal, state, or county position."** That's not a preference, it's baked
into the minimum qualification itself, under both the experience-only path and the
education-and-experience path. Josh has zero public-sector (federal, state, or county)
experience anywhere in the profile — his entire background is private-sector SaaS,
MarTech, and agency work. Education can substitute for the general years-of-experience
requirement, but the posting's phrasing ties the 2-year public-sector clause to the
qualifying experience itself, not to something education stands in for. Treat this as
effectively unmet unless Josh has government analyst experience not yet in the profile.

State HR screening here is unusually literal: only the online application's work-history
fields are reviewed for minimum qualifications ("your experience will not be inferred or
assumed"), resume and cover letter are recommended attachments, not substitutes. A
tailored resume can't work around that; the actual gatekeeping happens in the
application's own experience-detail fields, and answering there honestly would show the
same gap.
