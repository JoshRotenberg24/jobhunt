# Application Answers — Manager, Customer Marketing, PDQ

## Why PDQ?

PDQ's core value of ownership, taking responsibility for outcomes and following through
on commitments, describes how I already work: at Solenzo I run the full customer
lifecycle myself, from onboarding through win-back and reactivation, because I own the
outcome end to end, not just a piece of it. That's the operating mode this role calls
for.

The specific work maps closely too. I've built the lifecycle mechanics this role owns:
automated onboarding nurture, win-back, and reactivation sequences on top of defined
customer segmentation and lifecycle-stage frameworks. At Wix, I managed a team of
onboarding specialists for two years (20, then 8) while my own accounts drove a 30%
increase in traffic and a 25% lift in conversion. At Birdeye, I managed a reseller
partner book and retained 94% of it by staying close to partner health metrics and
intervening early.

I'm also drawn to how PDQ frames its 4-day week, not as a perk bolted on, but as a real
operating choice. That kind of intentionality about how work gets done is the same thing
I look for in a team before I join it.
