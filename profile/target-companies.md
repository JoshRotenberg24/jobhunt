# Target Companies — Proactive Outreach List

> Companies where Josh's actual experience (not a generic "marketing" match) crosses
> over directly with what the company does or sells. Built from the Work History,
> Bullet Bank, and Core Competencies sections of `profile/master-profile.md`. Meant for
> cold outreach, informational interviews, and LinkedIn networking — not tied to a
> specific open req the way `applications/` entries are.

**Before reaching out:** re-check the **Job-search knockouts** in
`profile/master-profile.md` (remote US or Denver/CO metro only, English only) against
each company's actual work-location policy — that can change company to company and
year to year, so verify current policy rather than assuming from this list.
Headquarters/product details below reflect general knowledge and may have shifted;
confirm before an outreach message leans on a specific fact.

Company already in `applications/` are excluded here to avoid duplication.

**Priority tier: Colorado-headquartered companies (Category 9).** These carry zero
work-location risk — no need to verify a remote policy, and a Denver/Boulder-metro HQ
means a warm intro or an in-person coffee is realistic in a way it isn't with a
remote-first company elsewhere. Worth working this category first.

## How to use this

- **Lead with the specific overlap, not a generic pitch.** "I sold and implemented a PSA platform to agencies for two years" lands harder at Kantata or Scoro than a generic marketing-ops intro.
- **Channel/partnerships and customer-marketing roles** (categories 1, 2, 9) are the
  strongest match to the Birdeye reseller-management track record.
- **Implementation, onboarding, and customer-success roles** (categories 2, 3, 4, 5)
  match the Wix and Birdeye onboarding record directly.
- **Growth marketing / marketing ops roles** (categories 1, 6, 7) match the Solenzo
  work most directly, especially anywhere the JD names GoHighLevel, HubSpot, or
  workflow/automation building explicitly.
- **Category 7 (AI agent tooling)** is worth prioritizing for outreach even without an
  open req — Josh is a genuine builder in this exact space, which is rarer than
  general marketing-ops experience.
